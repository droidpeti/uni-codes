n+1. row (n > 1):

In row n we inspect the origin vertexes of the optimized distances:
Do they optimize the distance further?
If Vertex A's distance d(A) gets better in step number n and A -> B edge exists (where the distance is c between A and B), then we
update d(B) to min(d(B, D(A) + c)). And is d(B) got smaller, then we update p(B) to A (where p(B) is the parent of B)
